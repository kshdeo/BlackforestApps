About Memorable

Memorable is a personal information management app designed to help you organize and access the important details of your life in one secure place. From medical records to travel documents, from emergency contacts to insurance information — keep everything organized and accessible when you need it most.

Key Features

📋 Organized Categories

Create custom categories to organize information exactly how you need it.

🔒 Secure Storage

Your data is stored securely on your device with optional iCloud sync.

📄 Document Management

Store photos and documents alongside your information for easy access.

🏥 Health Information

Dedicated sections for medical records, medications, and emergency contacts.

✈️ Travel Ready

Keep travel documents, itineraries, and important numbers in one place.

🌍 Region Aware

Emergency numbers automatically match your current region.

Privacy Policy

Overview

Memorable is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Storage

Memorable stores your information locally on your device. You have the option to sync your data to iCloud for backup and accessibility across your Apple devices. All data remains under your control.

Data Collection

Memorable only collects the information you explicitly enter into the app. We do not collect:

  • Your location (except optionally for emergency number localization)
  • Your contacts or health data (unless you choose to add it)
  • Analytics or usage data
  • Any personally identifiable information beyond what you provide

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account. Apple manages iCloud according to their privacy policy, and we cannot access your synced data.

Third Parties

Memorable does not share your data with any third parties. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

Security

Your data is protected by device-level security and optional iCloud encryption. We recommend:

  • Using a strong device passcode
  • Keeping your iOS version up to date
  • Enabling iCloud backup for redundancy

App Permissions

Memorable requests the following permissions only as needed:

  • Photos: To add photos to your records
  • Camera: To take photos for your records
  • Health: To access health information (optional)
  • Contacts: To link to your contacts (optional)

All permissions are optional and can be managed in your device settings.

Data Deletion

You can delete any information from the app at any time. Uninstalling the app will delete all local data. If you use iCloud sync, you may need to manually delete data from iCloud.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Updates will be reflected on this page.
