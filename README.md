## Apps

### Memorable
A personal information management app for organizing life's important details securely.

**Features:**
- Organized categories for different types of information
- Secure local storage with optional iCloud sync
- Document management with photos and files
- Health information tracking
- Emergency number localization
- Travel document organization

**Privacy First:** All data is stored locally on your device. Learn more in our [Privacy Policy](https://kshdeo.github.io/BlackforestApps).
